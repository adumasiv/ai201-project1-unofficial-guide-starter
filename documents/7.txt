Red Lion Apartments are fully gated, roomy for the price, and maintenance is awesome at getting things done ASAP. Office staff is so kind. There’s a bike path right across the street that goes to downtown Ypsilanti and leads to Frog Island Park, and there are dollar stores and restaurants (fast food and otherwise) within walking distance. There is a free shuttle run by the apartment place that takes people to EMU and WCC on the weekdays. In the summer, management has various local food trucks come out that park in the parking lot and sell food! Really great place to live!
