Some fire options that haven't been mentioned: Dos Hermanos has a relatively new place downtown, La Torre on Washtenaw does traditional Mexican foods like posole on the weekends but also the only place I know of to get a tongue burrito on the weekdays. Pita Pita is great if you're on the West end of town for Mediterranean and Caspian is pretty good if you're over on the south side (I'm assuming you're at Eagles Crest Marriott because I'm pretty sure that's our only chain hotel)
